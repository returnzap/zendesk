Subject: Refund notification email shows wrong line item on multi-item fulfillments (returnProcess mutation)

Hi Shopify,

We're reporting a bug where the refund notification email sent to customers displays the wrong line item after a refund is processed via the returnProcess GraphQL mutation. Our merchant has confirmed this has happened multiple times.

---

SUMMARY

When we call the returnProcess mutation with notifyCustomer: true to refund a specific ReturnLineItem, the customer-facing refund notification email displays a different line item from the same fulfillment instead of the one that was actually refunded.

---

REPRODUCTION CASE

Shop: Daisy Jewellery London
Order: #382123 (gid://shopify/Order/13078499066238)
Return: gid://shopify/Return/78198407550 (382123-R1)
Refund: gid://shopify/Refund/1188751737214
Date: March 26, 2026 ~14:20 UTC

The order had one fulfillment (382123-F1) containing two items:
  1. Polly Crystal Stacking Ring 18ct Gold Plate (LineItem/37460377141630, SKU: SPR13_GP_S) - GBP 89.10
  2. Polly Crystal Huggie Hoop Earrings 18ct Gold Plate (LineItem/37460377174398, SKU: SPE26_GP) - GBP 89.10

The customer returned the RING. We processed the refund via returnProcess with:
  - returnLineItems: [{id: "gid://shopify/ReturnLineItem/124487270782", quantity: 1}]
  - The ReturnLineItem maps to FulfillmentLineItem/19274362421630, which maps to LineItem/37460377141630 (the ring)
  - financialTransfer amount: GBP 89.10
  - notifyCustomer: true

The mutation succeeded with no userErrors. The refund of GBP 89.10 was correctly applied.

However, the refund notification email the customer received showed the EARRINGS as the refunded item, not the ring.

---

EXACT MUTATION INPUT

mutation {
  returnProcess(input: {
    returnId: "gid://shopify/Return/78198407550",
    returnLineItems: [{
      id: "gid://shopify/ReturnLineItem/124487270782",
      quantity: 1
    }],
    exchangeLineItems: [],
    refundShipping: {shippingRefundAmount: null, fullRefund: false},
    notifyCustomer: true,
    financialTransfer: {
      issueRefund: {
        allowOverRefunding: false,
        orderTransactions: [{
          transactionAmount: {amount: "89.1", currencyCode: GBP},
          parentId: "gid://shopify/OrderTransaction/14597178917246"
        }],
        refundMethods: []
      }
    }
  }) { ... }
}

---

EXPECTED BEHAVIOR

The refund notification email should show "Polly Crystal Stacking Ring 18ct Gold Plate" as the refunded item, since that is the item associated with the ReturnLineItem we specified.

ACTUAL BEHAVIOR

The refund notification email showed "Polly Crystal Huggie Hoop Earrings 18ct Gold Plate" as the refunded item.

---

ADDITIONAL NOTES

- The merchant reports this has happened multiple times across different orders.
- Both items were in the same fulfillment (382123-F1) and had the same price (GBP 89.10). The notification may be selecting the wrong line item from the fulfillment when both items have identical refund amounts.
- The refund itself was applied correctly (correct amount, correct transaction). Only the notification email content is wrong.
- Our app ID: 1229084
- Shopify API version: 2025-07
